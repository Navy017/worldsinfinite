Getting around

Look around

Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom. The map wraps east to west like a globe. Keep zooming: the land, then towns, then streets load in.

The bar at the top left shows where you are. Click any part of it to jump there.

Find anything

Press / and type: a realm, town, province, culture, faith, zone, sea, war, monument or person.

Atlas is the one place for everything: nations, stories, wars, history, trade, mysteries, wonders, people, and wildlife on land and at sea.

Click things

Provinces, towns, markets, armies (then their divisions down to single soldiers), ships, caravans, travellers, animals, monuments, roadside inns and border posts all have cards. Cards have buttons to go further: the realm's codex, its market, its zone.

Countries and people

Click any province, then Codex of … for that country: overview, government and court, key people, culture, faith, army, economy, science, history and its chronicle. Atlas → Nations lists every country and Atlas → People every ruler and notable. Search finds rulers, armies, fleets, ships, animals, libraries and markets by name.

Secrets

Every world hides one or two deeper truths. Pieces of them are kept in libraries, temples, ruins, village tales, inns and people, and many are biased, partial or wrong. Open a province and look for Secrets and old texts; the Journal (J) gathers what you find and gives leads.

Time

The clock at the bottom runs the world. 1mo and 1yr let history move quickly: wars, rulers, discoveries, storylines. Chronicle lists what happened.
